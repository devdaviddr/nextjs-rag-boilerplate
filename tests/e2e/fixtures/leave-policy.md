# Leave policy

This policy explains the leave you are entitled to.

## Annual leave

Full-time staff receive 25 working days of annual leave per calendar year.
Leave must be approved in advance by your line manager.

## Parental leave

Parents receive 18 weeks of paid parental leave.

| Type            | Weeks |
| --------------- | ----- |
| Primary carer   | 18    |
| Secondary carer | 4     |
