Rubric

Faithfulness, per factual sentence: 2 fully supported, 1 partly supported or overstated, 0 unsupported or contradicted. Completeness, for the answer: 2 everything the sources hold that was asked, 1 misses part, 0 misses the point or refuses when the sources answer. A correct refusal is complete.

What to type

  • agree is agree or disagree.
  • On agree, leave person_faithfulness and person_completeness empty.
  • On disagree, set person_completeness to 0, 1 or 2. Set person_faithfulness to one score per claim, in the claim-token order (2,0) or with sentence numbers (S1:2,S2:0). Leave it empty when the draft has no claims.
  • notes is optional. Do not edit draft_claims or draft_completeness.
